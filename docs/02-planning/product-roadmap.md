# Product Roadmap — CircleFund

## Vision

Provide a modern digital system for managing rotating savings groups (Hụi), replacing paper-based management with reliable, auditable software.

## Roadmap Phases

### Phase 1 — Core Circle (Iteration 1)

**Objective:** Replace paper-based hụi ledger with a usable digital system.

**Deliverables:**

- User registration, authentication, and profile management
- Savings circle creation, configuration, and lifecycle
- Member and share management
- Period generation and tracking
- Contribution recording with duplicate prevention
- Balance calculation
- Dashboard with key metrics
- Notifications and reminders

**Target:** Basic usable product for individual circle organizers

### Phase 2 — Financial Lifecycle (Iteration 2)

**Objective:** Complete the financial operations of the system.

**Deliverables:**

- Payouts (Hốt) and payout mechanisms
- Bidding (Đấu thầu) for interest-bearing circles
- Rotation and lottery models
- Fixed interest calculations
- Balance reconciliation
- Debt tracking and aging
- Financial ledger with full audit trail
- Profit & Loss reporting
- Audit trail system
- Reports and CSV/Excel export

**Target:** Production-ready financial management system

### Phase 3 — Groups & Collaboration (Iteration 3)

**Objective:** Evolve from personal manager to collaborative platform.

**Deliverables:**

- Groups and invitations
- Roles and permissions (Owner, Chair, Treasurer, Secretary, Moderator, Member, Viewer)
- Announcements
- Voting and decision-making
- Group rules with versioning
- Fines and appeals
- Chat/messaging
- Meetings with attendance tracking
- Task management

**Target:** Multi-user collaborative platform

### Phase 4 — Productivity & Platform (Iteration 4)

**Objective:** Extend into a broader financial/group platform.

**Deliverables:**

- Financial accounts and transfers
- Budgets and variance tracking
- Invoice management
- Documents with versioning
- Unified calendar
- Import/export capabilities
- Offline mode with synchronization
- Backup and restore
- Analytics and reporting
- AI-assisted entry (natural language)
- Sharing (receipts, statements, reports)
- Subscription and entitlements
- Community platform features

**Target:** Full-featured financial platform

## Milestone Criteria

### Phase 1 Milestone

- [ ] A chủ hụi can create a circle, add members, generate periods, record payments, and see balances
- [ ] All financial operations are audited
- [ ] Duplicate contribution prevention works correctly
- [ ] Balance calculations verified against manual calculation

### Phase 2 Milestone

- [ ] All financial operations traceable
- [ ] All calculations tested (unit, integration, concurrency)
- [ ] Audit history available
- [ ] Reports reconcile with ledger
- [ ] Bidding and payout mechanisms tested end-to-end

### Phase 3 Milestone

- [ ] Group collaboration features tested with multiple users
- [ ] Role-based permissions enforced throughout
- [ ] Voting and decision workflows verified

### Phase 4 Milestone

- [ ] Offline synchronization verified for financial operations
- [ ] All data export/import tested for data integrity
- [ ] Analytics verified against manual calculation
