# Product Roadmap — Vision & Structure

**Index:** `docs/02-planning/product-roadmap.md`

Provide a modern, offline-first digital system for managing rotating savings and credit associations (ROSCAs / Hụi).

```text
Phase 1  Offline-First Core ROSCA Management
Phase 2  Financial Operations (Offline-Capable)
Phase 3  Integrations (External Services)
Phase 4  Group Collaboration & Governance
Phase 5  Advanced Platform
```

Offline reliability is built into the core from day one; integrations have their own phase; collaboration/platform arrive after the core is stable.
