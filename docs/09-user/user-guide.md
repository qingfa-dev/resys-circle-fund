# User Guide — CircleFund

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Navigate to the CircleFund homepage
2. Click "Register"
3. Enter your details:
   - Username or email
   - Password (must meet complexity requirements)
   - Confirm password
4. Check your email for a verification link
5. Click the verification link to activate your account
6. Log in with your credentials

### Logging In

1. Navigate to the login page
2. Enter your username/email and password
3. Click "Login"
4. You will be redirected to your dashboard

### Security Tips

- Use a strong, unique password
- Enable two-factor authentication if available
- Log out when using shared devices
- Never share your login credentials

## Navigating CircleFund

### Dashboard

Your dashboard is your central hub. It displays:

- **Active Circles** — Your current savings circles
- **Upcoming Periods** — Periods with approaching due dates
- **Unpaid Contributions** — Contributions you still owe
- **Total Balance** — Your overall financial position
- **Recent Activity** — Latest transactions and updates

### Savings Circles

#### Viewing Circles

From the dashboard, click on any circle to view its details:

- Circle name, type, and status
- Current period and next due date
- Member list and contribution progress
- Quick actions: Add contribution, View ledger, View reports

#### Creating a Circle

1. Click "New Circle" from the Circles page
2. Enter circle details:
   - **Name:** Descriptive name for your circle
   - **Type:** Non-interest, Fixed Interest, or Bidding
   - **Start Date:** When the circle begins
   - **Contribution Amount:** How much each member pays per period
   - **Schedule:** Frequency and due date
   - **Number of Periods:** How many rounds (or indefinite)
3. Click "Create Circle"
4. Add members to your circle

#### Managing Circle Settings

As the Circle Organizer, you can:

- Edit circle configuration
- Pause/resume the circle
- Close completed circles
- Manage member permissions
- View circle history and audit trail

## Core Operations

### Recording a Contribution

1. Navigate to the circle page
2. Click "Record Contribution"
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Member:** Select the member making the payment
   - **Period:** Select the contribution period
   - **Amount:** Enter the contribution amount (auto-filled from circle settings)
   - **Payment Date:** Date the payment was received
   - **Payment Method:** Cash, bank transfer, e-wallet, etc.
   - **Reference:** Optional external reference number
   - **Note:** Optional note about the payment
4. Review the summary
5. Click "Confirm" to record the contribution
6. The system will update balances and create ledger entries automatically

### Viewing Payment Status

1. Navigate to the circle page
2. Go to the "Members" or "Periods" tab
3. View each member's payment status:
   - **Unpaid** — No payment recorded
   - **Partial** — Partial payment made
   - **Paid** — Full payment received
   - **Overdue** — Payment past due date
   - **Waived** — Payment waived by organizer
   - **Cancelled** — Payment cancelled

### Viewing Financial Ledger

1. Navigate to the circle page
2. Click "Ledger" tab
3. View all financial transactions:
   - Contributions (incoming)
   - Payouts (outgoing)
   - Interest calculations
   - Adjustments and reversals
4. Filter by date range, transaction type, or member
5. Click any entry to view details
6. Export to CSV or Excel using the "Export" button

### Checking Balances

Each circle provides balance information at three levels:

- **Member Balance:** Individual member's outstanding and completed obligations
- **Period Balance:** Collection progress for the current period
- **Circle Balance:** Overall financial position of the circle

Balances are always calculated from actual transactions (ledger entries), not manually edited values.

## Notifications

CircleFund can send notifications for:

- Upcoming contribution due dates
- Overdue payments
- Period opening/closing
- Payout results
- Circle changes
- System announcements

Manage your notification preferences in Settings.

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Cannot log in | Check credentials, click "Forgot Password" if needed |
| Feature not working | Refresh page, clear browser cache, check for updates |
| Wrong balance displayed | Refresh the page; balances update after each operation |
| Contribution rejected | Check validation messages, ensure period is open and member is active |
| Cannot access circle | Ensure you are a member of the circle or have appropriate permissions |
