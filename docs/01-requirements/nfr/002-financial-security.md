# NFR-002 — Financial Security

**Category:** Financial Security
**Applies:** I1–I5

**Statement:** Financial operations shall require authorization and validation.

**Sub-requirements:**
- NFR-002.1 Financial mutations shall be authenticated.
- NFR-002.2 Financial mutations shall be authorized.
- NFR-002.3 Sensitive financial mutations shall be audited.
- NFR-002.4 Duplicate financial operations shall be prevented.
- NFR-002.5 Financial corrections shall be traceable.
