# NFR-029 — Offline Reliability

**Category:** Offline Reliability
**Applies:** Critical I1/I2; ✓ I3; — I4/I5

**Statement:** When offline functionality is enabled:
- NFR-029.1 Local operation status shall be visible.
- NFR-029.2 Local operations shall have unique identifiers.
- NFR-029.3 Synchronization shall be retryable.
- NFR-029.4 Conflicts shall be detected.
- NFR-029.5 Conflicts shall not be silently discarded.

Financial operations require stricter synchronization rules than ordinary collaborative content.
