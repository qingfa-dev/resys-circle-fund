# NFR-030 — AI Safety and Correctness

**Category:** AI Safety
**Applies:** Critical at I3

**Statement:** For AI-assisted financial entry:
- NFR-030.1 AI output shall be treated as a proposal.
- NFR-030.2 The proposed transaction shall be shown to the user.
- NFR-030.3 User confirmation shall be required.
- NFR-030.4 The original interpreted input shall be traceable.
- NFR-030.5 AI failure shall not prevent manual entry.
