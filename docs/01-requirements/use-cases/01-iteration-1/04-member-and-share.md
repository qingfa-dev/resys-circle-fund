# Member & Share — Use Cases

**Iteration:** 1 — Offline-First Core ROSCA Management
**SRS:** `docs/01-requirements/srs.md` §3.1.4

## UC-I1-021 — Add Member
**Actor:** Circle Organizer. Provide info → validate → add.

## UC-I1-022 — View Member
**Actor:** Circle Organizer / Circle Member.

## UC-I1-023 — Suspend Member
**Actor:** Circle Organizer. Suspend → status change.

## UC-I1-024 — Remove Member
**Actor:** Circle Organizer. Remove → check implications.

## UC-I1-025 — Assign Share
**Actor:** Circle Organizer. Assign → validate.

## UC-I1-026 — Assign Multiple Shares
**Actor:** Circle Organizer. Assign multiple → validate constraints.

## UC-I1-027 — Transfer Share
**Actor:** Circle Organizer. Transfer → validate → record.

## UC-I1-028 — View Membership History
**Actor:** Authorized User.

## Associated User Stories
- US-I1-010 — *As a Circle Organizer, I want to add a member so that the member can participate in the circle.*
- US-I1-011 — *As a Circle Organizer, I want to assign one or more shares to a member so that their obligations can be calculated correctly.*
- US-I1-012 — *As a Circle Member, I want to see my membership and share information so that I understand my participation.*
- US-I1-013 — *As a Circle Organizer, I want to suspend or remove a member so that membership reflects the actual state of the circle.*
