# Reconciliation & Audit Rules — Business Rules

**Category:** Reconciliation & Audit

## BR-REC-001 — Scope
Reconciles expected (config-driven) vs. actual (ledger) amounts per round.

## BR-REC-002 — Discrepancy
Adjustments require authorization and are recorded as ledger entries.

## BR-AUD-001 — Coverage
Audits financial mutations, circle lifecycle changes, member changes, permission changes, and configuration changes.

## BR-AUD-002 — Content
Actor, action, timestamp, resource, before/after, correlation ID, reason.
