# Payout Draw & Bidding Rules — Business Rules

**Category:** Payout Draw & Bidding

## BR-PAY-001 — Eligibility
Eligible when holding an active share, the round is open, and contribution requirements are met (unless rules allow otherwise).

## BR-PAY-002 — Single Recipient
One recipient per round. In Bidding circles the winner is the highest bidder (per configured rule).

## BR-PAY-003 — Rotation
Recipients rotate among eligible members; no member receives twice until all have (unless configured).

## BR-BID-001 — Window
Bids accepted only while bidding is open.

## BR-BID-002 — Minimum
Bid must meet the configured minimum.

## BR-BID-003 — Winner
Determined by configured rule (highest/lowest/custom).

## BR-BID-004 — Uniqueness
New bid replaces prior (prior preserved for history).
