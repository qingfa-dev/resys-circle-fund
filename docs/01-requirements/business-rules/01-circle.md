# Circle Rules — Business Rules

**Category:** Circle (Savings Circle)

## BR-CIR-001 — Ownership
A circle has exactly one Circle Organizer at all times; ownership transfers to another authorized member if the organizer is removed.

## BR-CIR-002 — Lifecycle
`Active → Paused → Active`; `Active → Closed → Archived`. Contributions cannot be recorded for a Closed circle.

## BR-CIR-003 — Configuration
A circle is configured with Name (unique per organizer), Association Type, Start Date, contribution schedule, Number of Rounds (or indefinite), Number of Shares, Contribution Amount, Rules.

## BR-CIR-004 — Types
No-Interest Rotation, Fixed Interest, Bidding.

## BR-CIR-005 — Minimum Members
At least 2 members before the first round opens.
