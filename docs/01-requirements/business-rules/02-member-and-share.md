# Member & Share Rules — Business Rules

**Category:** Member & Share

## BR-MEM-001 — Membership
A member may belong to multiple circles; holds the permitted number of shares per circle.

## BR-MEM-002 — Status Lifecycle
`Active → Suspended → Active`; `Active → Removed`. Suspended members cannot contribute but retain shares.

## BR-MEM-003 — Share Assignment
Shares must be assigned before the first round opens; post-open changes require authorization and audit.

## BR-MEM-004 — Transfer
Share transfer follows circle rules and is traceable.
