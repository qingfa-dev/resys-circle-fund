# Subscription/Billing Integration — Functional Requirements

**Iteration:** 3 — Integrations (External Services)
**SRS:** `docs/01-requirements/srs.md` §3.3.6

## FR-I3-023

**Statement:** The system shall maintain subscription plans via the connected Subscription Provider.

## FR-I3-024

**Statement:** The system shall associate entitlements with subscriptions.

## FR-I3-025

**Statement:** Expired subscriptions shall be handled according to plan rules.

## FR-I3-026

**Statement:** Subscription state changes shall be recorded.

**Related:** UC-I3-023..026, US-I3-010/011, NFR-002 (entitlement checks at authorization)
