# Community — Functional Requirements

**Iteration:** 5 — Advanced Platform
**SRS:** `docs/01-requirements/srs.md` §3.5.9

## FR-I5-028

**Statement:** Authorized users shall be able to create community posts.

## FR-I5-029

**Statement:** Users shall be able to view posts according to visibility rules.

## FR-I5-030

**Statement:** Moderators shall be able to moderate content.

## FR-I5-031

**Statement:** The platform may maintain a business/service directory.

**Related:** UC-I5-030..035, US-I5-015..017
