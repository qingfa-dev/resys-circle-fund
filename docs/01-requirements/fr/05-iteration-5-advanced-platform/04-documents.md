# Documents — Functional Requirements

**Iteration:** 5 — Advanced Platform
**SRS:** `docs/01-requirements/srs.md` §3.5.4

## FR-I5-012

**Statement:** Users shall be able to upload documents where authorized.

## FR-I5-013

**Statement:** The system shall store document metadata.

## FR-I5-014

**Statement:** The system shall support document versions.

## FR-I5-015

**Statement:** Access to documents shall respect permissions.
