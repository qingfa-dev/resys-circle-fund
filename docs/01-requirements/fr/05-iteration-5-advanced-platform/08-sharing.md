# Sharing — Functional Requirements

**Iteration:** 5 — Advanced Platform
**SRS:** `docs/01-requirements/srs.md` §3.5.8

## FR-I5-025

**Statement:** Authorized users shall be able to share supported records.

## FR-I5-026

**Statement:** Shared information shall respect privacy and permissions (NFR-018).

## FR-I5-027

**Statement:** The system shall provide a shareable representation (Receipt, Statement, Report, Document).

**Related:** US-I5-014, NFR-018
