# Budget Management — Functional Requirements

**Iteration:** 5 — Advanced Platform
**SRS:** `docs/01-requirements/srs.md` §3.5.2

## FR-I5-005

**Statement:** Users shall be able to create budgets.

## FR-I5-006

**Statement:** Budgets shall support: Period, Category, Target amount, Actual amount, Variance.

## FR-I5-007

**Statement:** The system shall calculate budget utilization.
