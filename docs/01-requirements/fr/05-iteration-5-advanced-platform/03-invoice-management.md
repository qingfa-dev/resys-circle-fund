# Invoice Management — Functional Requirements

**Iteration:** 5 — Advanced Platform
**SRS:** `docs/01-requirements/srs.md` §3.5.3

## FR-I5-008

**Statement:** Authorized users shall be able to create invoices.

## FR-I5-009

**Statement:** Invoices shall have statuses: Draft, Issued, Paid, Partially Paid, Overdue, Cancelled.

## FR-I5-010

**Statement:** Invoices shall support line items.

## FR-I5-011

**Statement:** Invoice history shall be preserved.

**Related:** UC-I5-010..014, US-I5-006/007
