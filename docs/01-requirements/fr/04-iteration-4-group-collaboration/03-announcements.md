# Announcements — Functional Requirements

**Iteration:** 4 — Group Collaboration & Governance
**SRS:** `docs/01-requirements/srs.md` §3.4.3

## FR-I4-010

**Statement:** Authorized users shall be able to publish announcements.

## FR-I4-011

**Statement:** Announcements shall support: Title, Content, Author, Created time, Expiration, Attachments.

## FR-I4-012

**Statement:** Users shall be able to view announcement history.

**Related:** UC-I4-011..015, US-I4-006
