# Roles & Permissions — Functional Requirements

**Iteration:** 4 — Group Collaboration & Governance
**SRS:** `docs/01-requirements/srs.md` §3.4.2

## FR-I4-006

**Statement:** The system shall support group roles (Owner, Chair, Treasurer, Secretary, Moderator, Member, Viewer).

## FR-I4-007

**Statement:** The group owner shall be able to assign roles.

## FR-I4-008

**Statement:** The system shall enforce role-based permissions.

## FR-I4-009

**Statement:** Permission changes shall be auditable (BR-AUD-001).

**Related:** UC-I4-007..010, US-I4-004/005, NFR-001/002
