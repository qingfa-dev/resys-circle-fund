# Fines & Appeals — Functional Requirements

**Iteration:** 4 — Group Collaboration & Governance
**SRS:** `docs/01-requirements/srs.md` §3.4.6

## FR-I4-022

**Statement:** Authorized users shall be able to assign penalties/fines according to group rules.

## FR-I4-023

**Statement:** The system shall record: Member, Violation, Rule, Amount, Date, Actor, Evidence, Status.

## FR-I4-024

**Statement:** Members shall be able to appeal.

## FR-I4-025

**Statement:** Authorized users shall be able to review appeals.
