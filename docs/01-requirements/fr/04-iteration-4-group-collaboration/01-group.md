# Group — Functional Requirements

**Iteration:** 4 — Group Collaboration & Governance
**SRS:** `docs/01-requirements/srs.md` §3.4.1

## FR-I4-001

**Statement:** A user shall be able to create a group.

## FR-I4-002

**Statement:** A group shall have: Name, Description, Owner, Members, Roles, Rules, Status.

## FR-I4-003

**Statement:** A group owner shall be able to invite users.

## FR-I4-004

**Statement:** Invitees shall be able to accept or reject invitations.

## FR-I4-005

**Statement:** A group owner shall be able to remove members where authorized.

**Related:** UC-I4-001..006, US-I4-001..003, NFR-001 (authorization)
