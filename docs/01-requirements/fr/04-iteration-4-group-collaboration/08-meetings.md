# Meetings — Functional Requirements

**Iteration:** 4 — Group Collaboration & Governance
**SRS:** `docs/01-requirements/srs.md` §3.4.8

## FR-I4-031

**Statement:** Authorized users shall be able to schedule meetings.

## FR-I4-032

**Statement:** The system shall store: Title, Agenda, Date/time, Location/link, Participants.

## FR-I4-033

**Statement:** The system shall record attendance.

## FR-I4-034

**Statement:** Authorized users shall be able to record meeting minutes.

**Related:** UC-I4-037..041, US-I4-016..018
