# Group Rules — Functional Requirements

**Iteration:** 4 — Group Collaboration & Governance
**SRS:** `docs/01-requirements/srs.md` §3.4.5

## FR-I4-019

**Statement:** Authorized users shall be able to create group rules.

## FR-I4-020

**Statement:** Rules shall have version/history.

## FR-I4-021

**Statement:** Rule changes shall record the actor and timestamp.
