# Communication — Functional Requirements

**Iteration:** 4 — Group Collaboration & Governance
**SRS:** `docs/01-requirements/srs.md` §3.4.7

## FR-I4-026

**Statement:** Members shall be able to send group messages.

## FR-I4-027

**Statement:** Users shall be able to reply to messages.

## FR-I4-028

**Statement:** Messages shall retain sender and timestamp.

## FR-I4-029

**Statement:** The system may support voice notes.

## FR-I4-030

**Statement:** Message permissions shall respect group membership.

**Related:** UC-I4-032..036, US-I4-013..015
