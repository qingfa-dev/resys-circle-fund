# Reconciliation — Functional Requirements

**Iteration:** 2 — Financial Operations (Offline-Capable)
**SRS:** `docs/01-requirements/srs.md` §3.2.5

## FR-I2-021

**Statement:** The system shall reconcile expected and actual amounts for each round (BR-REC-001).

## FR-I2-022

**Statement:** The system shall identify discrepancies.

## FR-I2-023

**Statement:** The system shall require authorization for adjustments (BR-REC-002).

## FR-I2-024

**Statement:** Reconciliation results shall be recorded.

**Related:** UC-I2-020..024, US-I2-008, BR-REC-001/002
