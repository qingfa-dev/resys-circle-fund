# No-Interest Rotation / Lottery — Functional Requirements

**Iteration:** 2 — Financial Operations (Offline-Capable)
**SRS:** `docs/01-requirements/srs.md` §3.2.3

## FR-I2-012

**Statement:** The system shall support a non-interest rotation model.

## FR-I2-013

**Statement:** The system shall maintain payout order (BR-PAY-003).

## FR-I2-014

**Statement:** The system shall prevent a member from being selected twice when configured rules prohibit it.

## FR-I2-015

**Statement:** The system shall optionally support a lottery-based selection process.

## FR-I2-016

**Statement:** Lottery results shall be permanently recorded.

**Related:** UC-I2-012..016, US-I2-005/006, BR-PAY-003
