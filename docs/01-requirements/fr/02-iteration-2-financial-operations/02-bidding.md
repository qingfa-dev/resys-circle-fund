# Bidding — Functional Requirements

**Iteration:** 2 — Financial Operations (Offline-Capable)
**SRS:** `docs/01-requirements/srs.md` §3.2.2

## FR-I2-006

**Statement:** The system shall allow eligible members to submit bids.

## FR-I2-007

**Statement:** The system shall enforce bid rules configured for the circle (BR-BID-001/002).

## FR-I2-008

**Statement:** The system shall prevent invalid or duplicate bids (BR-BID-004).

## FR-I2-009

**Statement:** The system shall determine the winning bid according to configured rules (BR-BID-003).

## FR-I2-010

**Statement:** The system shall preserve bid history.

## FR-I2-011

**Statement:** The winning result shall be linked to the corresponding round.

**Related:** UC-I2-006..011, US-I2-003/004, BR-BID-001..004, NFR-005 (Concurrency)
