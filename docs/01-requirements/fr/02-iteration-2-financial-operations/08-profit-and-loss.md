# Profit & Loss — Functional Requirements

**Iteration:** 2 — Financial Operations (Offline-Capable)
**SRS:** `docs/01-requirements/srs.md` §3.2.8

## FR-I2-034

**Statement:** The system shall calculate estimated financial results.

## FR-I2-035

**Statement:** The system shall calculate actual financial results based on recorded transactions.

## FR-I2-036

**Statement:** The system shall distinguish income, expense, payout, and other financial categories where applicable.

**Related:** UC-I2-033..035, US-I2-013/014
